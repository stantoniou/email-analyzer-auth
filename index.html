How it works

Three Steps to Better Email Management

Up and running in minutes — no configuration, no IT department needed.

1
Install & Connect

Add the extension to Chrome or Edge and sign in with your Microsoft 365 account. Secure OAuth 2.0 authentication — your password never touches us.

2
Automatic Analysis

Pick a start date and hit Scan. The extension reads your inbox and sent mail, counts every interaction, and ranks every contact by email volume.

3
Find Anyone, Fast

Search across all emails instantly. Click any contact to see your complete conversation history — sent and received — in one clean view.
