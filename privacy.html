Section 1

Overview & Scope

This Privacy Policy describes how Staviox ("we", "us", or "our"), a brand operated by Integrationhub Consulting CY LTD (a company registered in Nicosia, Cyprus), handles information in connection with the Email Contact Analyzer browser extension ("the Extension").

This policy applies to all users of the Extension worldwide. By installing or using the Extension you acknowledge that you have read and understood this policy.

🛡️
Privacy-first by design The Email Contact Analyzer processes all data exclusively on your device. No personal information is collected by Staviox, stored on our servers, or shared with any third party other than Microsoft's own services, which you authorise directly via OAuth consent.
Section 2

Data We Collect

The Extension reads the following data from your Microsoft Outlook account solely to operate its features on your device. None of this data is transmitted to Staviox or any server we operate.

Category Examples Purpose
Email Metadata Sender address, recipient(s), subject line, date & time Rank contacts by email volume; display communication analytics
Email Body Preview First 255 characters of the email body Enable full-text search across your mailbox within the Extension

ℹ️

Section 3

How We Use Your Data

All data is used exclusively to provide the features of the Extension. Specifically:

  • To display your contacts ranked by email communication volume.
  • To power search functionality across email subjects and body previews.
  • To generate email pattern analytics visible only to you within the Extension.
  • To retain your display preferences (theme, window position, sort order) between browser sessions.
  • To maintain an authenticated session with the Microsoft Graph API so the Extension can retrieve your email data.
✅
100% client-side processing Every computation — contact ranking, search indexing, analytics — happens inside your browser. Staviox never receives, processes, or stores any of this information.

Section 7

Security Measures

We have implemented the following technical safeguards to protect your data within the Extension:

  • OAuth 2.0 with PKCE — Authentication uses the industry-standard Proof Key for Code Exchange flow, protecting against authorisation code interception attacks.
  • Secure token storage — OAuth access tokens are stored in browser sessionStorage, scoped to the current session and never persisted across sessions.
  • No password storage — The Extension never requests, handles, or stores your Microsoft account password.
  • iframe sandboxing — Email HTML content is rendered inside a sandboxed <iframe> to prevent cross-site scripting (XSS) attacks originating from email content.
  • Input escaping — All user-supplied input is escaped before rendering to prevent injection attacks.
  • HTTPS-only — All communication with Microsoft Graph API and Azure AD is conducted exclusively over HTTPS/TLS-encrypted connections.
ℹ️
While we implement robust client-side security measures, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure. We encourage you to keep your browser and operating system up to date and to protect your Microsoft account with a strong password and multi-factor authentication.
Section 8

Third-Party Services

The Extension interacts exclusively with the following Microsoft services, each of which has its own privacy policy and terms of service:

Microsoft Graph API

Used to retrieve your Outlook email data (metadata, subject lines, body previews, and contact information) using the OAuth access token you grant. Data is fetched directly from Microsoft to your browser — Staviox does not receive or store this data.

Microsoft Azure Active Directory (Azure AD)

Used solely to facilitate the OAuth 2.0 PKCE authentication flow that grants the Extension permission to access your Outlook data on your behalf. Your credentials are handled entirely by Microsoft.

The Extension does not use any other third-party services, including but not limited to advertising networks, analytics platforms, crash-reporting services, or social media SDKs.

We encourage you to review Microsoft's Privacy Statement at privacy.microsoft.com to understand how Microsoft processes your data.

Section 9

Microsoft Graph API & Permissions

The Extension uses the Microsoft Graph API exclusively to read your Outlook email and contact data on your behalf. The following OAuth permission scopes are requested at the time of authentication:

Permission Scope What It Allows Why We Need It
Mail.Read Read emails in your mailbox (metadata and body) Fetch email metadata, body previews, and sender/recipient information for contact ranking and search
Contacts.Read Read your saved Outlook contacts Supplement email-extracted contacts with data from your Outlook contact list
User.Read Read your basic profile information (name, email address) Identify the signed-in user and personalise the Extension interface
🔐
Minimal permissions, maximum transparency We request only the permissions necessary to operate the Extension's features. No write permissions are requested — the Extension can only read your data, never create, modify, or delete emails, contacts, or any other Outlook content.

Key principles governing our use of the Microsoft Graph API:

  • Data retrieved via the API is used solely for the purposes described in this policy and is never shared with Staviox or any third party.
  • No data is shared with Microsoft beyond what is inherent in standard API usage (i.e., the API requests themselves).
  • You remain in full control of the Extension's access to your Microsoft account at all times. You may revoke these permissions at any time via myapps.microsoft.com or your Microsoft account settings.
  • All API calls are made over HTTPS and follow Microsoft's recommended security practices.
Section 10

Children's Privacy

The Email Contact Analyzer is a professional productivity tool designed for adult users and is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect or process any personal information from children under the age of 13. Because all processing happens locally in the browser and no data is transmitted to Staviox, we do not maintain records about our users. However, if a parent or guardian believes that a child under 13 has used the Extension, they are encouraged to:

  • Uninstall the Extension from the child's browser to remove all locally stored data immediately.
  • Revoke the Extension's OAuth access via the child's Microsoft account settings.
  • Contact us at support@staviox.com to request any further assistance or clarification.

We will respond to all parental data deletion requests promptly and take all appropriate steps within our technical capabilities.

Section 11

GDPR Compliance

Integrationhub Consulting CY LTD is headquartered in Nicosia, Cyprus, a member state of the European Union. We are committed to complying with the General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679 ("GDPR").

Because all data is processed and stored locally on your device and no personal data is transmitted to or processed by Staviox, the primary data controller relationship under the GDPR for your email data is between you and Microsoft. Nonetheless, the Extension is designed in full accordance with GDPR principles:

  • Data minimisation — We access only the data required to provide the Extension's features (email metadata and the first 255 characters of the body for search).
  • Purpose limitation — Data accessed is used solely for the purposes described in this policy and no other purpose.
  • Storage limitation — Data persists only as long as the Extension is installed or until you clear it via browser settings.
  • Integrity and confidentiality — Appropriate technical measures (OAuth PKCE, HTTPS, iframe sandboxing, input escaping) are implemented to protect data security.
  • Right to erasure — You may erase all Extension data by uninstalling the Extension or clearing browser storage.
  • Right of access — All Extension-held data is visible directly within the Extension's interface.
  • Lawful basis — Processing is based on your explicit consent, given when you authorise the Extension via Microsoft's OAuth consent screen.

For any GDPR-related enquiries, please contact us at support@staviox.com.

Section 13

International Users

The Email Contact Analyzer is available to users worldwide. Because all data is processed locally in your browser, there are no cross-border data transfers initiated by the Extension.

  • Local processing only — Your email data is fetched from Microsoft's servers directly into your browser and is never routed through Staviox or any infrastructure we operate.
  • No cross-border transfers — Since Staviox does not receive your data, no transfer of personal data across international borders occurs on our part.
  • EU users (GDPR) — The Extension is designed in compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation. EU users enjoy all GDPR rights as described in Section 11.
  • California users (CCPA) — We do not sell personal information. California residents have the right to know about and delete personal information we hold — as no data is held by us, these rights are satisfied by default.
  • Other jurisdictions — We are committed to respecting applicable privacy laws in all regions. Because data never leaves your device (beyond standard Microsoft API communication), the Extension's privacy model is inherently compliant with local-processing requirements across most jurisdictions.
🌍
Microsoft's data residency When the Extension fetches your emails via the Microsoft Graph API, that data is handled by Microsoft's infrastructure subject to Microsoft's own data residency and international transfer policies. Please refer to Microsoft's Privacy Statement for details.
